// About ↓ scroll

Started with pencils, not keyboards. Before any line of code, I was sketching mechanisms, trying to understand how things move and why. That instinct led me into robotics, then into deep learning, and now into AI agents that operate autonomously in high-stakes security environments.

I've worked across the full stack of intelligent systems — from commissioning 48 industrial robots on factory floors, to building physics engines from scratch, to fine-tuning foundation models for robotic manipulation. I don't just apply methods; I reach for the right tool & build it if it doesn't exist.

What drives me is the belief that the most interesting problems sit at the boundary — where perception meets action, where autonomy meets safety, where art meets engineering. That's where I work best.
